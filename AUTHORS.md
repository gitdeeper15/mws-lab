## Conceptual and Methodological Foundations

The research draws on five intersecting domains:

### 1. Environmental and Earth Sciences
- Freshwater resource assessment.
- Water scarcity indicators (Falkenmark, water-stress index).
- Climate and rainfall variability.
- Groundwater depletion and dam storage dynamics.

### 2. Agricultural and Water Policy
- Agricultural water demand.
- Irrigation efficiency.
- Water reuse and desalination capacity.
- National water strategy (Morocco's 2025 State of the Environment Report).

### 3. Statistics and Econometrics
- Ordinary least squares regression.
- Multicollinearity diagnostics (VIF, condition number).
- Confidence intervals and hypothesis testing.
- Correlation analysis (Pearson matrix).

### 4. Data Science and Reproducibility
- World Bank WDI data extraction.
- Reproducible analysis pipeline (Python 3.9+).
- Versioned datasets and fixed seeds.

### 5. Science Communication and Open Research
- Open data licensing (CC-BY-4.0 for World Bank data).
- Open manuscript (CC-BY-4.0).
- Open code (MIT).
- Zenodo DOI deposit for permanent citation.
